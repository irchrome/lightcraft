## 4. Infrared Mixes Between sRGB Primaries (`ir_mix`)
- **Commands:** `develop.mixerSwapRBsRGB` ("Swap Red and Blue (sRGB)") and `develop.mixerIrMix {mix}` ("More IR mixes": R↔G, G↔B, two rotations, R↔B 70% / 30%, mono from red, plus 38 extended clean-room 3×3 mixes synced from `ir-profiles-hub` exposed via the `IR Mix Catalog` flyout in `Infrared` and the `IR-FALSE-COLOR` / `IR-MONO` sections in `Edit`). Switching camera profiles (`develop.profile`) automatically resets any active swap/channel mixer in the same undo step (`reset_swap_on_profile_change` in `crates/engine/src/cmd/ir.rs`, `reset_swap_preview` in profile hover previews).
- **Math & UX:** `P·S·P⁻¹·Base` between sRGB primaries (Rec.2020 swap turns gold pre-swap sky teal, h ≈ 216° vs ≈ 251°). Clicking a mix **replaces** the active IR mix (never stacks — prevents bug of 2026-11-12 on "Mono from Red"); clicking active mix again returns to `Base`; external mixer edits become new `Base`. Stored in `DevelopSettings::ir_mix` (`crates/develop/src/ir_mix.rs`, omitted from JSON when unset). Results past ±200% scale down and report `darker`.
- **Fork-owned files:** `crates/pipeline/src/ir_swap.rs`, `crates/pipeline/src/ir_mix_catalog.rs` (generated by `ir-profiles-hub`), `crates/pipeline/assets/ir_mix_catalog.json`, `crates/engine/src/cmd/ir.rs`, `crates/ui-egui/src/panels/ir_mix.rs`, `crates/ui-egui/src/panels/ir_edit.rs`, `crates/develop/src/ir_mix.rs`.
- **Upstream files touched (re-apply after PR #3579 re-squash):**
  - 1 line each in `crates/pipeline/src/lib.rs`, `crates/ui-egui/src/panels.rs`, `crates/ui-egui/src/panels/edit.rs` (plus `sections()` call in `edit.rs`), `crates/ui-egui/src/panels/profiles.rs`, `crates/ui-egui/src/panels/edit/sliders.rs`

  - 3 lines in `crates/ui-egui/src/menus/ui_commands.rs` (`section.irFalseColor`, `section.irMono`, `section.irAerochrome`)
  - locale keys at end of `crates/ui-egui/locales/*.json`

## 5. Camera-Bound DCP Profiles & Lightroom Metadata Recovery (`ir_profiles`, `ir_lightroom_meta`)
- **Behavior:** imported `.dcp` is offered and accepted by `develop.profile` only for a raw matching `UniqueCameraModel` (`dcp_fits`: lowercase alphanumeric whole names, make + model, never prefixes). Paste, sync, Auto Sync, and presets keep a photo's own profile when carried profile doesn't fit. LUT profiles remain universal.
- **Lightroom import & fallback recovery:** `ir_lightroom_meta.rs` harvests `AgInternedExifCameraModel`, `AgInternedExifLens`, and `AgHarvestedExifMetadata` plus XMP `tiff:Make`/`tiff:Model` during `.lrcat` import; `recheck::stale` probes raw stubs with empty `meta.camera`; `photo.relink` and virtual copies run `fill_missing_meta`; `ir_profiles.rs` falls back to a cached 64 KiB EXIF header read for on-disk raws and offers labeled DCPs under `"Camera Profiles (camera unknown)"` for manual selection on offline unknown-camera raws (while blocking Sync/Paste/Auto Sync).
- **Fork-owned files:** `crates/engine/src/ir_profiles.rs`, `crates/engine/src/ir_lightroom_meta.rs`.
- **Upstream files touched:** 2 lines in `crates/engine/src/lib.rs` (`mod ir_lightroom_meta;`, `pub mod ir_profiles;` + `keep_own_camera_profile` in `Session::develop_op`), 1 in `crates/engine/src/cmd/develop.rs` (`refuse_unfit`), 1 in `crates/engine/src/presets.rs` (`imported_profiles_for` / `append_dcp_groups`), 6 in `crates/engine/src/lightroom_catalog.rs`, 4 in `crates/engine/src/cmd/recheck.rs`, 3 in `crates/engine/src/cmd/missing.rs`, 2 in `crates/engine/src/cmd/convert.rs`, 3 in `crates/ui-egui/src/menus/run.rs` (`find_missing_done`), 2 in `crates/ui-egui/src/panels/profiles.rs`, 2 in `crates/ui-egui/src/panels/edit/sliders.rs`, fixture helper in `crates/engine/src/tests/dcp.rs`.

## 7. Soft Gamut Compression for IR Edits (`ir_gamut`)
- **Behavior (owner decision 2026-11-13, IR photos only):** ACES Reference Gamut Compression (threshold 0.7, limit 2.2, power 1.2) runs when photo has user DCP (`dcp:` id) or non-neutral channel mixer, before mixer zero-clamp and output gamut map. Prevents flat yellow/cyan fills in foliage-balanced IR skies. Ordinary photos unaffected.
- **Files:** `crates/pipeline/src/ir_gamut.rs`; GPU twin `ir_compress` in `crates/gpu/src/wgsl/finish.wgsl`, `IR_GAMUT`/`IR_GAMUT_S` in `crates/gpu/src/params.rs`, `FinishParams::ir_gamut`; CPU = GPU within 1 code value (`equivalence.rs`). Does not bump `RENDER_CACHE_VERSION`: rebuild IR previews with `library.buildPreviews ids=[…] force=true`.

## 9. Dedicated Infrared Tab in `Tools` (`ir_tab`) & `Edit` IR Sections (`ir_edit`)
- **Behavior:** `[ Edit | Infrared ]` segmented tab bar in the `Tools` panel (`RightPanel::Edit` + `UiState::ir_tab`, 0 lines in `docking.rs`, `detail.rs`, and `strip.rs`). Groups camera DCP quick list with live `HoverPreview` + `Amount`, WB (`Temp`/`Tint`, `W` picker) + compact `Exposure`/`Vibrance`/`Saturation`, and Infrared Channel Mixer (neutral picker, `Swap Red and Blue (sRGB)`, `More IR mixes`, `IR Mix Catalog` with family filters and live `HoverPreview`, Reset, 3×3 sliders). Inside `Edit` (`panels/edit.rs`), `ir_edit::sections` prepends three dedicated collapsible sections at the top of `Edit`: `IR-FALSE-COLOR` (camera-bound IR DCP quick list, WB/neutral pickers, 46 sRGB false-color mixes, vibrance/saturation), `IR-MONO` (monochrome mixes + tone/contrast), and `AEROCHROME` (Chrome/Aerochrome camera DCP quick list, WB/neutral pickers, Foliage & Sky HSL, Primary Calibration).
- **Fork-owned files:** `crates/ui-egui/src/panels/ir_tab.rs`, `crates/ui-egui/src/panels/ir_edit.rs`.
- **Upstream files touched:** `crates/ui-egui/src/panels.rs` (`pub mod ir_tab;`), `state.rs` (`ir_tab: bool`), `panels/right.rs` (tab bar & dispatch), `panels/edit.rs` & `panels/edit/sliders.rs` (`prepared_develop` + `pub(crate)` visibility), `menus/ui_commands.rs` / `menus/menubar.rs` / `menus/run.rs` (`panel.infrared`), locale keys at end of `crates/ui-egui/locales/*.json`.
